Angle convention: 0° = toward Mic A (left), 90° = straight ahead, 180° = toward Mic B (right), 270° = behind. Two mics can't tell front from back, so the ray stays in the front half-plane. With Mic C the azimuth covers the full 0–360°. Mic positions: x right, y ahead, as "x,y x,y x,y" for A B C; blank = equilateral triangle with C ahead. Calibrate with a clap or clicks at the cal azimuth. The spectra shade the band below the 120 Hz high-pass.
